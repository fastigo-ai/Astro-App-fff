📱 Complete Course eCommerce Mobile App Integration

This command center manages everything live on the app: courses, coupons, live classes, kit deliveries, and module gating without needing app redeployments.

1. Coupons & Promotions

Coupons created here can be applied at checkout via POST /api/v1/admin/coupons/verify, computing instant discounts on orders.

2. Live Masterclasses

Webinars scheduled here sync with the app's ClassesScreen.tsx so expecting parents can join directly via Zoom or Google Meet.

3. Dynamic Gating & Locking

The app's ModuleAccessGate checks the user's active scheme to selectively lock or unlock kick counters, 3D baby bumps, and Garbh Samvad.

4. Physical Kit Dispatch

When users purchase premium packages, kit shipments (saffron, books, sound kits) are tracked here with courier numbers.
